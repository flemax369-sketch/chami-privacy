Privacy Policy — Chami - Img to PDF

Last updated: 9 November 2026. App: Chami - Img to PDF (package: com.chami.pdfmaker).

1. Your files stay on your phone

We do NOT collect, upload, store, share or sell your PDFs, images, scanned documents or resumes. All processing — Images to PDF, PDF to Images, Split, Merge, Compress, Passport Photo, Image Resizer, Scanner, OCR and Resume Builder — happens on your device.

2. No account needed

We do NOT ask for an account, email address, phone number or password. No signup is required to use the app.

3. Permissions

We request only the permissions needed for the feature you use: Camera (to scan documents), Photos / Media (to pick images), and Storage (to save PDFs on your device). You can deny a permission — that feature will not work, but the rest of the app will.

4. OCR

Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device. Text from your documents is not sent to any server by this app.

5. Ads and payments

Version 1 shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. There is no Pro payment. The Pro Pack (all resume templates, no watermark) is unlocked for free by sharing the app with 3 friends from inside the app. No payment details are collected.

6. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data deletion

Because your files stay on your device, deleting the app deletes all your app data. There is no account or server copy to delete.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and update the policy inside the app.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us from inside the app: Settings > Help / Privacy Policy.
